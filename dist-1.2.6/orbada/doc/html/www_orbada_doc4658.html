1.2.1.319, release: 2014-07-13, Production/Stable

  • Added
  • #329 (fr) - PostgreSQL: alter column on view
  • #328 (fr) - PostgreSQL: on table list, information about the inheritance
  • #327 (fr) - Plug-in for snippets
    For each editor SQL/Java and for each driver, you can now create snippets.
    Browse them in plugins settings.
    There are snippets that you can not edit, they are (|) {|} [|], '|', "|", `|` - you will not see them but they exist and act immediately.
    Your snippets, if are not "immediate" resolve after pressing tab.
